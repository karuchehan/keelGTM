# keelGTM — Complete Project Context

## What keelGTM Is

keelGTM is a B2B lead infrastructure company. We build ICP-matched account and prospect lists for B2B sales teams, enriched, validated, and handed off clean. One engagement, no retainers, no dependencies.

The name comes from the keel of a ship — the structural fin beneath the hull that keeps everything stable and on course. The keel is what makes everything else work. That is what keelGTM is for outbound: the infrastructure beneath the GTM motion.

---

## The Two Offerings

### 1. The Keel Build

The main done-for-you service. End-to-end list building for B2B sales teams.

**What it covers:**
- Account list building — finding the right companies that match the client's ICP
- Account list enrichment — adding data layers to each account
- Prospect list building — finding the right people at those accounts
- Prospect list enrichment — validating, enriching, and completing each prospect record

**How it works:**
Clients can buy the full end-to-end stack or just the part they need. If they already have an account list, we build the prospect list. If they have prospects but need enrichment, we do that. The full stack is the complete offering.

**The add-on: Signal-Based Personalization**
On top of the list build, clients can add personalization. We pull signal intelligence — hiring signals, funding signals, tech installs, executive movements, and other buying triggers — and produce personalization points and lines for their outbound campaigns. This turns a cold list into a campaign-ready asset.

**The positioning:**
We build the lead infrastructure their outbound runs on. Fast, precise, handed off clean. Not a retainer. Not an agency relationship. One engagement, one deliverable, they own it from day one.

**The locked headline:**
"The right accounts. The right people. Built fast, handed off clean."

**The locked subtext:**
"We build ICP-matched account and prospect lists from scratch, enriched, validated, and ready for outbound. One engagement, no retainers, no dependencies."

### 2. Prospect Validation Tool

A separate product built to solve a specific problem: manual prospect validation is slow and does not scale. The tool automates 80% of the prospect validation process.

**Full technical details:** See `prospect_validation_tool.html` in the `/docs/` folder.

**How the funnel works — two tiers:**

**Tier 1: Pay-per-run — $49**
The hook. Anyone can come to the website, pay $49, upload a list of 250 prospects, and get back a validated, verdict-ready output. No setup required. No commitment. They experience the product firsthand before spending anything significant. This is the lead magnet that does the selling.

**Tier 2: Full setup — $1,000 (shown as discounted from $1,500)**
After they have experienced the $49 run and seen the output quality, the upsell is the full setup. We build and configure the entire system for them, one time, and they own it forever. Shown on the site at $1,500 with a clear deadline discount to $1,000 to create urgency. Price will increase as demand grows.

**Who buys the tool:**
In-house GTM engineers, list builders, RevOps teams, and ops people who want to build their own prospect lists faster without outsourcing the whole thing.

**The logic:**
The $49 run is not a revenue play. It is a trust and demonstration play. It makes the $1,000 feel obvious because they have already seen what the output looks like. The $49 run does the selling for us.

## Copywriting Rules

These apply to every single piece of copy in every file at all times. No exceptions.

- **No em dashes.** Never use — anywhere. Use a comma, a period, or rewrite the sentence.
- **No filler words.** Never use: seamless, leverage, streamline, cutting-edge, innovative, robust, powerful, game-changing, best-in-class, world-class, unlock, elevate.
- **No corporate speak.** Write the way a sharp, direct person talks. Short sentences. Plain English.
- **No exclamation marks** in copy. No ellipses for dramatic effect.
- **Active voice always.** Never passive voice.
- **Specificity over vagueness.** "250 prospects validated in one run" not "fast results."
- **Headlines are statements, not questions.**
- **One idea per sentence.** If a sentence has two ideas, split it into two sentences.

## Positioning Framework

Drawn from the $100M Leads methodology (see `hormozi_leads.md`):

**The core offer positioning:**
The Keel Build is not a list building service. It is lead infrastructure. The deliverable is not data. The deliverable is pipeline that moves. The list is the mechanism. The outcome is outbound that actually works.

**The lead magnet play:**
The $49 pay-per-run on the Prospect Validation Tool is the lead magnet for the $1,000 full setup. Solve a narrow problem cheaply, deliver insane value, reveal the bigger problem, offer the bigger solution. Classic problem-solution cycle from Hormozi.

**The value equation applied to keelGTM:**
- Dream outcome: a clean, enriched, campaign-ready list of the right accounts and people, fast
- Perceived likelihood: real named projects, real methodology, real credibility from xGrowth work
- Speed: one engagement, not months of work
- Ease: they do not touch Clay, do not figure out enrichment waterfalls, do not waste their team's time

**Social proof strategy:**
No fake logos. No invented testimonials. Lead with proof of craft instead. Show the actual methodology, the actual builds, the actual outputs. The Playbook section exists for this purpose. Real named projects: Shopify ANZ ABM list, Nerdio tech stack identification, Epicor ANZ ABM. These are case studies of how we think and build, not client testimonials.
