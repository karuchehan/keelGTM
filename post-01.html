ABM Principle 01

You're reaching out to accounts that aren't ready to buy. That's why nothing is working.

Most outbound fails because of timing, not copy. Here is what ABM figured out about buying windows that high-volume outbound has never caught up to.

The Timing Problem

When most teams run outbound campaigns, they assume that the buyer is ready to hear from them. They assume that because they've identified the right company and the right person, all they need to do is craft a compelling message and the deal will follow.

This is where high-volume outbound gets it fundamentally wrong.

What ABM Understands That Outbound Doesn't

Account-Based Marketing teams learned something crucial that most outbound organizations haven't internalized: timing is everything. Not just timing in the sense of "send at the right time of day." Timing as in: is the buyer actually in a buying window?

ABM teams invest heavily in understanding when an account is actively looking to solve a problem. They're watching for signals. They're tracking changes in the organization. They're monitoring for initiatives that create a problem the prospect can't ignore.

And they only engage when those signals appear.

The Cost of Bad Timing

When you reach out to an account that isn't ready to buy, three things happen:

  • You train the prospect to ignore you. Every cold message that lands when they don't have a problem is just noise.
  • You waste your SDR's time. They're chasing opportunities that don't exist yet, or exist elsewhere in the organization.
  • You pollute your data. You don't know if they didn't reply because they're not interested, or because they're not ready yet.

How to Fix This

The fix isn't to send better emails. The fix is to wait for the signal. Identify what signals indicate a buying window for your ICP. Are they searching for specific keywords? Posting jobs in certain departments? Going through leadership changes? Running earnings calls that mention problems you solve?

Once you know what to look for, only reach out when you see those signals. This is how ABM-thinking outbound actually wins.
